# 12. Research and Medical Guidelines — FootGuard AI

## Standards Followed
1. **IWGDF 2023 Guidelines**: International Working Group on the Diabetic Foot recommendations for risk stratification, daily inspection, and protective footwear.
2. **WHO 2023 Standards**: World Health Organization guidelines on non-communicable disease (NCD) management and early amputation prevention.
3. **ICMR 2023 Guidelines**: Indian Council of Medical Research guidelines for management of Type 2 Diabetes in India.

## Pathology & Staging (`DFUInformation.tsx`)
- Meggitt-Wagner Ulcer Staging System (Grade 0: Pre-ulcerative lesion to Grade 5: Extensive gangrene).
- Sensory Neuropathy (loss of protective sensation) vs Ischemia (peripheral artery disease) differentiation.
