# 01. Project Overview — FootGuard AI

## Introduction
FootGuard AI is an intelligent clinical decision-support and early prevention system designed for Diabetic Foot Ulcer (DFU) screening. It combines a real machine learning binary classifier trained on patient skin patches with Gemini vision-based foot validation and a dynamic voice-enabled AI nurse ("Paathasuvadu").

## Key Objectives
- **Early Detection**: Provide rapid, non-invasive screening for diabetic foot ulcers to prevent severe complications, infection, and amputations.
- **Accessibility**: Support dual-language interaction (English and Tamil) tailored for patients across Tamil Nadu.
- **Evidence-Based Care**: Adhere to international guidelines including IWGDF 2023, WHO 2023, and ICMR standards.

## Target User Base
- Patients with Type 1 or Type 2 Diabetes performing daily foot monitoring at home.
- Community health workers and rural caregivers in Tamil Nadu requiring decision-support tools.
